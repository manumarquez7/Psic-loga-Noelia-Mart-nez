## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an empathetic, comforting, and deeply nurturing environment tailored for child and adolescent psychological care. It blends soft organic warmth with professional credibility, offering parents and young patients an immediate sense of relief, emotional safety, and validation.

The design movement combines **Gentle Organic Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Softness**:
- **Atmosphere:** Cloud-like serenity, warm maternal tenderness, gentle guidance, and open listening.
- **Visual Tone:** Uncluttered, generous breathing space, soft rounded boundaries, and pill-shaped elements that eliminate harsh angles or intimidating clinical rigidness.
- **Audience Connection:** Designed primarily for mothers, fathers, guardians, and adolescents seeking a confidential, safe, and professional clinical space where feelings are embraced without judgment.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from tender childhood memories, therapeutic watercolor tones, and calming nursery warmth. It relies on a high-comfort light mode using creamy off-whites instead of stark digital whites.

- **Primary (`#E0838A` / Tint `#FFC9C9`):** A warm, dusty rose that communicates empathy, heartbeat, love, and emotional connection. Used for primary CTAs, active highlights, and emotional milestones.
- **Secondary (`#89C9C5` / Tint `#B7E5E3`):** A soothing soft mint-cyan representing calmness, anxiety reduction, breath, and intellectual freshness.
- **Tertiary (`#A99DE3` / Tint `#D8CFF8`):** A subtle lilac evoking introspection, gentle imagination, youth psychology, and emotional exploration.
- **Surface & Backgrounds (`#FFFDF9` & `#FFF6E9`):** Buttercream and warm oat tones that replace cold clinical whites with an enveloping, home-like tactile foundation.
- **Neutral Text (`#4A3E3D`):** A deep, comforting espresso-charcoal that maintains WCAG AAA readability against pastel surfaces without the jarring harshness of `#000000`.
- **Accent WhatsApp (`#25D366`):** Reserved strictly for direct appointment booking and instant contact actions, set in rounded floating containers.

## Typography

The typography system relies exclusively on **Nunito Sans** to deliver rounded terminals, humanist proportions, and an inherently approachable disposition. 

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with higher weights (600–800) to communicate authority and medical competence while remaining friendly and completely devoid of aggressive terminal edges.
- **Body & Explanatory Prose:** Set with generous line heights (1.6x) to facilitate stress-free reading for parents navigating delicate situations like behavioral challenges, grief, school adaptation, and anxiety.
- **Microcopy & Credentials:** Clean, warm uppercase tracking for licenses (e.g., `MP 885...`), building instantaneous confidence without formal sterility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an airy, humanized fluid grid with wide gutters and generous vertical breathing room to cultivate tranquility:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max container width capped at `1160px` to maintain focused readability. Utilizes an asymmetrical 12-column layout that pairs narrative text blocks alongside organic, rounded card containers. Section margins are expansive (`4rem` to `6rem`) to prevent sensory overload.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with grouped 2-column service cards and full-width testimonial pods.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** Single-column stack with generous horizontal margins (`1.25rem`), allowing thumb-friendly browsing with rounded elements floating gracefully over the `#FFFDF9` cream canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established using layered pastel planes and ultra-soft, diffused ambient shadows rather than harsh grey drop shadows:

- **Surface Layering:** The base page rests on Warm Cream (`#FFFDF9`). Elevated surfaces use Soft Biscuit (`#FFF6E9`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), or tinted pastel washes (`#FFC9C9` at 30% opacity, `#B7E5E3` at 25% opacity).
- **Ambient Tinted Shadows:** Shadows utilize warm umber and dusty rose tones (`rgba(164, 110, 110, 0.08)` to `rgba(90, 60, 60, 0.06)`) with high diffusion radii (`24px` to `40px`) and minimal vertical offset (`8px` to `16px`). This achieves a weightless, cloud-like lift.
- **Subtle Containment Borders:** When borders are needed for form fields or tertiary cards, they use soft semi-opaque outlines (`rgba(224, 131, 138, 0.2)` or `rgba(137, 201, 197, 0.3)`) that gently cradle the content.

## Shapes

The shape system is hyper-rounded and organic, reinforcing reassurance, maternal embrace, and playful childhood gentleness:

- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create smooth, touchable surfaces.
- **Cards & Content Pods:** Styled with ultra-generous radiuses (`rounded-3xl` / `24px` to `32px`), resembling smooth river stones, soft cushions, and friendly dialog bubbles.
- **Hero & Profile Imagery:** Housed inside perfect circular cutouts or soft asymmetrical organic squircular frames, echoing the heart and organic curves of the brand identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Agendar Consulta / Contactar):** Pill-shaped with a gradient or solid fill in Rose (`#E0838A`), white text, bold Nunito weight, accompanied by a soft rose hover glow (`box-shadow: 0 10px 24px rgba(224, 131, 138, 0.35)`).
- **WhatsApp Direct CTA:** Pill-shaped button or persistent floating bubble in `#25D366`, white icon and typography, offering immediate reassurance for parents seeking direct conversation.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped with an off-white or light mint fill (`#B7E5E3`), neutral text, and subtle hover tint.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Therapy Pods (Infantil, Juvenil, Orientación Familiar):** Built on `rounded-3xl` containers with creamy white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`), an ambient peach-rose shadow, and a gentle pastel accent header strip (e.g., `#B7E5E3`, `#D8CFF8`, `#FFC9C9`).
- **Internal Padding:** Roomy `2rem` (space-lg) padding providing calm spatial rhythm.

### Input Fields & Booking Forms
- **Fields:** Generously proportioned with pill or rounded-2xl geometry (`16px`), soft cream background (`#FFF6E9`), delicate neutral-taupe text, and a focused border transition to mint (`#89C9C5`) with no harsh focus rings.
- **Labels:** Gentle, conversational labels that invite openness (e.g., *¿Cómo podemos acompañarte?*).

### Chips & Badges
- **Specialty & Age Tags (e.g., "Infancias", "Adolescencia", "Crianza Respetuosa"):** Pill-shaped chips in soft pastel fills (`#D8CFF8` at 40% opacity, `#B7E5E3` at 50% opacity) with dark neutral text and comfortable horizontal padding (`1rem`).

### Testimonial & Empathy Quotes
- **Quote Cards:** Curved speech-bubble or cloud forms with oversized quotation marks in soft rose, showcasing parent testimonials, reassuring reflections, and therapeutic phrases that foster immediate trust.
